Offline world-map geometry

The bundled maps/world-countries.tsv file contains simplified country-boundary
geometry derived from Natural Earth, 1:110m Cultural Vectors, Admin 0 –
Countries dataset.

Dataset source:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/110m-admin-0-countries/

Natural Earth terms of use:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Natural Earth states that its data are public domain. The geometry is simplified
and transformed for this application's offline traffic-destination map. It is
not a navigational or boundary-authority dataset.
